Peter Parker never asked to be a legend.

Brilliant, broke and always running late, Peter patches his own suit, pays his debts in favors, and still stops to help a stranger with the groceries. Spider-Man is what happens when the kid next door refuses to look away.

Real name
Peter Benjamin Parker
Home
Forest Hills, Queens
First seen
Amazing Fantasy #15, 1962

Origin story

The field trip

A science exhibit, a class of bored teenagers, and one genetically altered spider drifting down on a thread nobody noticed.

The bite

A sting on the back of the hand. By morning the world is louder and brighter, and Peter's fingertips hold to the wall like they were made for it.

The mistake

He had the power to stop a thief and chose not to. The cost of that choice arrives the same night.

The promise

Uncle Ben's words become Peter's rule: with great power comes great responsibility. He sews a mask and goes out into the city.

Chapter 1 of 4

Everything the bite gave him, and everything he built himself.

Spider-sense

A tingle at the base of the skull, half a second before danger arrives. It does not say what is coming, only that it is.

Wall-crawling

Hands and feet that grip any surface, from glass towers to wet brick.

Superhuman strength

Enough to stop a speeding train, though it nearly breaks him.

Agility and reflexes

He reads a punch before it is thrown and treats the street like a gym.

Web-slinging

Web-shooters Peter designed himself. The proof that the brain is part of the power.

Every hero is measured by who stands against him.

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, a genius turned madman, with pumpkin bombs and a glider that never misses.

Doctor Octopus

Otto Octavius. Four mechanical arms fused to his spine, a scientist undone by his own invention.

Venom

An alien symbiote bonded to a bitter host. It knows every move Peter knows.

Sandman

Flint Marko becomes a storm of sand. More a desperate father than a born criminal.

Vulture

Adrian Toomes, a salvage man in a flying harness who turned a grudge into a business.

Electro

Max Dillon was nobody until a power surge made him current. Now he wants the whole city to notice.
